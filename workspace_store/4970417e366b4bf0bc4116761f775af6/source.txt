Every  year,  millions  of  children,  particularly  girls,  are  impacted  by  the  pervasive  worldwide  problem  of  child  marriage.  More  than  650  million  women  in  the  world  today  were  married  before  turning  18,  according  to  UNICEF.  An  estimated  12  million  girls  get  married  before  they  reach  adulthood  every  year.  This  practice  is  most  common  in  parts  of  South  Asia,  sub-Saharan  Africa,  and  some  parts  of  the  Middle  East  and  Latin  America,  though  it  is  found  throughout  the  world.
