Formalising the concept - discussion
• When two quantities are related in such a way that the 
increment (or decrement) of one quantity increases (or 
decreases) the other quantity at the same rate, we call 
the two quantities to be directly proportional.
• When two quantities are directly proportional to each 
other they are linked by a constant multiplier (known as 
the Constant of Proportionality). This means that the ratio 
of the two variables will always be this constant.

Formalising the concept - discussion
• When two quantities are related in such a way that the 
increase of one quantity causes a decrease in the other 
quantity at a proportional rate, we call the two quantities 
to be inversely proportional.
• When two quantities are inversely proportional to each 
other, the product of the two quantities will be a constant.
