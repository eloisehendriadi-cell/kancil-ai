🧠 Topic Overview
- Direct proportion expresses a relationship where one quantity increases or decreases at the same rate as another.
- Inverse proportion indicates that as one quantity increases, the other decreases at a proportional rate.
- The constant of proportionality links directly proportional quantities.
- Algebraic expressions can be used to find unknown quantities in both direct and inverse proportions.
- Real-life applications include scenarios like grocery pricing and workforce efficiency in construction.

🔑 Key Concepts
- Direct Proportion: A relationship where two quantities increase or decrease together at a constant rate.
- Inverse Proportion: A relationship where one quantity increases while the other decreases, maintaining a constant product.
- Constant of Proportionality: The constant multiplier that relates two directly proportional quantities.

🧬 Processes / Mechanisms
1. Identify the quantities involved (e.g., rice cost and weight).
2. Establish the relationship (direct or inverse).
3. Use algebraic expressions to calculate unknowns based on known values.
